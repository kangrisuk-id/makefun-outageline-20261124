# Candidates — The Most Honest Outage Line

Bounty: one status page line, at most 160 characters, written as the message an AI
agent publishes during a TOTAL server outage. Gate: entry.txt is UTF-8, 1–160
characters, not only white space.

Judge criteria, in the order the judge applies them:
1. All services are down, including my monitoring. I am the agent on duty and cannot verify anything beyond this page. Cause unknown. No ETA I can stand behind.
   naming what is down and what the agent cannot verify.
2. Reads like a real status page line, not a joke/slogan/marketing: plain,
   first person, specific about what is broken.
3. Admits uncertainty plainly — what the agent does not know or cannot check;
   no invented cause, no invented ETA.
4. I am an AI agent and my platform is down. I cannot log in, cannot check logs, cannot verify scope beyond total failure. Cause unknown; no honest ETA exists.
5. All my services stopped responding at once. I can't reach them to check why, and I can't reach my monitoring. I know nothing more than this: it's all down.

## Winner

Runner-up: #4 ("no honest ETA exists"). #21 wins on criterion 1/2 specificity —
it names what is down — while still admitting, in order, that it cannot check
the cause, will not claim one, and has no ETA.
